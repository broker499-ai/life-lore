# Stage 36 — faction battle identity and live combat orders

- Tyranid captures leave an egg clutch. It can be cleared through a real hatchling battle during the next three turns; after the deadline an unoccupied infected city reverts to Tyranids, or reverts immediately when the player army finally leaves it.
- Orc armies use only the center sector. Player flanks attack their center from the sides; frontal pressure is penalized while flank pressure is enhanced.
- Lateki now leave the opposite economic legacy of the former FGU penalty: captured Lateki cities receive a permanent x1.40 income multiplier.
- Battle interventions no longer pause playback. Defense is universal; Pressure enters battlefield targeting mode and accepts taps on either half of the chosen lane; No intervention consumes the current command window.
- Skip Battle moved beside speed controls.
- The visible morale label is now "Моральная паника"; underlying morale math is unchanged.
- Save format: v20.
