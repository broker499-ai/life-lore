# Stage 42 — recruitment rebuild, unit roster and living flanks

Version: 0.42.0
Save: v23

## Recruitment
- Fixed the Stage 41 recruitment sheet: slider and confirmation button are both visible; the button no longer overlays the slider.
- Recruitment is more generous. Each city offers Freshmen plus two seeded-random standard groups; the starting city offers all standard groups.
- The starting city can recruit remotely to the main army with 3-turn travel time and has a boosted safe quota that recovers after 6 turns.
- Unique recruits are seeded into random cities and require at least one artifact. Sirius Morpheus Nan is an optional seeded boss and joins after defeat.

## Units
Standard: Freshmen, Mirpolovtsy, Economists, Olympiadniks, Initiative Group, Philosophers.
Unique: Sirius Morpheus Nan, Greg Jenkins + growing spider horde, Xiang, Marconi, The Boys.

## Battle
- Live flank orders can be given once per battle round: own left/center/right can engage the corresponding or adjacent enemy sector.
- Circle positions visibly bend toward the selected target.
- Seeded panic dice can break a single sector before the others; weakened/routed formations dim and get dashed outlines.
- Xiang arrives late with a dedicated visual event and destroys an enemy flank.
