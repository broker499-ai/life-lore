# Current state — Stage 40

Package: `0.40.0`  
Save format: `v22`

Current campaign additions:
- supplyless movement is possible and cannot soft-lock a POI route;
- zero-supply travelling causes small end-of-turn troop losses outside friendly cities;
- POIs provide a one-use short rest;
- recruitment uses an amount slider with a visible safe threshold and seeded riot risk beyond it;
- failed risky recruitment causes a light battle and a 5-turn recruitment lock in that city;
- player recruitment is temporarily limited to one unit type pending the future unit-system redesign;
- battle impact banners persist longer and blood is guaranteed to become visible at least once per battle;
- Lateki and Tyranids use pink blood effects.
