# Stage 37 — opening descent cutscene

- A new expedition now starts with an intro cutscene after leader confirmation and before the campaign map mounts.
- The supplied vertical artwork scrolls automatically from its top to its bottom.
- At the bottom the scene dims lightly and reveals the lines:
  - «Корень...»
  - «Нам нужен Корень Живознания...»
- The sequence then enters the campaign automatically after a short reading hold.
- First tap/click anywhere reveals a bottom «Пропустить заставку» button; pressing that button skips immediately.
- Continue/load does not replay the cutscene.
- Portrait and phone-landscape layouts are supported; landscape keeps the vertical artwork centered with atmospheric side fill instead of stretching it.
- The original PNG was converted to a 682×2048 WebP (~267 KB) for mobile/PWA loading.
- Save format is unchanged (v20).
